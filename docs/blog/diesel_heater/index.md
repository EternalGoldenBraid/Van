# Diesel Heater — Planar 44D Relocation

**Status (2026-10-25):** Installation in progress.

## Setup

- **PLANAR-44D-12-GP-P**, 12 V, 4 kW, removed from the Transit during underside
  cleaning.
- Initial purpose: dry the van interior. May later replace with a ~2 kW heater.
- Reusing the existing fuel pump, line and as much of the exhaust as possible.

## Placement And Holes

- Relocating to the **sliding-door step well**, sideways, inspired by
  Mispronounced Adventures' MAN TGE / Crafter installation.
- Confirm the allowed sideways orientation against the Planar 44D mounting
  diagram before marking the holes.
- Aim for individual penetrations for fuel, combustion air and exhaust rather
  than the old large mounting-plate opening.
- Mark from the heater/template, then check underside access and drill.
- The reported holes are between 20 and 30 mm; a Bosch 6–30 mm step drill is
  selected, not purchased. Deburr with the existing files and coat the cut
  edges using the [shared primer/topcoat kit](#shared-coatings-and-preparation).

## Mounting And Exhaust

- Original backing plate cut off because the exhaust would not separate from
  the heater outlet; heater outlet left intact.
- Existing gasket retained; shallow marks at its outer edge.
- Original corrugated exhaust remains stuck to the heater. Plan is to split it
  farther downstream, near the muffler.
- Trial-fitted metal joining tube: ~22 mm ID, ~1 mm wall. Nominal OD would be
  24 mm; **measure actual OD** (23 mm was suggested in the review).
  Material/coating unknown.
- Final exhaust joint, clamps and mounting holes still to be completed and
  inspected.
