# Floor Insulation: The Next Work

**Working plan, 2026-10-23.** The next physical job is finishing the floor
insulation. This is its own stage of the van build, with a replacement-air
vent that also prepares for the future stove. This page is our working
instruction for the next stages; the steps below are planned, not completed.

## Finish The Floor Insulation

The order is:

1. **Remove the floor** to expose the steel and the old rivet holes.
   Keep the fitted insulation and floor pieces available for refitting.
2. **Seal the holes left by the removed rivets** using the existing
   {download}`floor-sealing plan <../../floor_sealing_logbook.md>`.
   That plan uses a bolt, washers and rubber seals on both faces of the floor,
   with local corrosion preparation first. Follow the coating's application
   and cure instructions and check leak-tightness before covering the repairs.
3. **Fit the stove's replacement-air vent: the "räppänä".**
   Resolve its location, opening size, route and fittings while the floor is
   accessible. Inspect both sides before cutting; avoid structural members,
   vehicle services and exhaust contamination. Protect and weather-seal the
   new cut edges and penetration using compatible materials.
4. **Apply closed-cell foam to the internal wheel arches.**
   Prepare clean, dry surfaces and use a compatible adhesive. Finish the
   floor-to-wheel-arch junctions while they are accessible; preserve factory
   drains and required access.
5. **Refit and finish the floor insulation** after the checks below.
   Complete the intended floor assembly before adding wall framing that
   prevents its removal.

The vent is an outside-air supply to the living space for the stove. A direct
connection to the stove has not been established. Its required free area and
arrangement still need checking against the stove's requirements; a grille or
mesh reduces the available opening. If it has a closure, the required air
supply must remain open while the stove is burning or embers remain. Do not
run the MaxxFan in extraction with a live stove without a designed and verified
depressurization arrangement.

**Before closing the floor:** the repaired holes must be dry and leak-tight,
coatings and adhesives sufficiently cured, the air vent fitted and unobstructed,
and the wheel-arch foam complete. Check whether the proposed stove mounting
will need underfloor access or backing plates, and preserve that access.
Record photos and measurements of anything that will be hidden.

The {download}`floor plan <../../floor_plan.md>` retains the floor-stack details.
The full stove installation plan comes next; this floor stage does not approve
the hearth, stove position or fire clearances.
