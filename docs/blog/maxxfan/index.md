# Roof Fan — MaxxFan Deluxe Installation

**Status (2026-10-25):** Work-package placeholder; parts and tools listed in the
[MaxxFan shopping list](shopping_list.md).

## Scope And Placement

- **Unit:** 400 x 400 mm MaxxFan Deluxe on the Ford Transit L3H3 roof.
- **Longitudinal position:** foremost sufficiently flat roof bay behind the
  front curved transition, clear of structural roof ribs (see
  {download}`roof layout plan <../../roof_layout_plan.md>`).
- **Lateral position:** centred or shifted toward the passenger side only as far
  as the receiving flange sits fully supported on the roof crown.
- **Make-up air (*korvausilma*):** shares the floor replacement-air inlet
  (*räppänä*) in [floor/replacement_air.md](../floor/replacement_air.md). Do not
  run the MaxxFan in exhaust mode while the wood stove is burning or embers
  remain.

## Installation Flow

1. **Measure and mark the opening**
   - Confirm roof-rib centres, interior ceiling framing and roof curvature in
     the front bay.
   - Mark the 400 x 400 mm cutout using the MaxxFan template; mask surrounding
     roof paint with painter's tape and set up interior swarf collection.
2. **Drill, cut and deburr**
   - Drill corner pilot holes, cut the sheet-metal opening with fine metal
     jigsaw blades, then file and sand (`P240`) all cut edges smooth.
   - Drill the flange fastener holes while catching all metal filings.
3. **Protect bare steel edges**
   - Clean the cut edge and drilled holes with isopropyl alcohol.
   - Brush-coat every bare metal edge and hole with corrosion paint
     (`Metallista 2-in-1`) and allow it to cure before bedding the flange.
4. **Bed and seal the receiving flange (butyl levelling method)**
   - Dry-fit the receiving flange over the opening.
   - Build up short strips of plain, non-reinforced RV butyl tape in the roof
     valleys until the flange sits flat without rocking.
   - Lay one continuous final butyl layer around the full flange perimeter.
   - Fasten the flange evenly by hand until butyl just begins to squeeze out;
     stop if the flange bows.
   - Cap every screw head and the entire flange perimeter with UV-resistant,
     plastic-compatible RV roof lap sealant.
5. **Fit interior support frame, 12 V feed and fan unit**
   - Install a structural timber/plywood interior support ring giving at least
     25 mm total roof thickness (XPS alone is not a structural clamping frame).
   - Run the fused 12 V supply (>= 5 A circuit), mount the MaxxFan body onto
     the receiving flange, verify lid operation, and fit the interior garnish
     ring.
