# Front seat swivels — VAN-46

**Status:** Planned. Parts, fitment, and costs not yet confirmed.
**Linear:** [VAN-46](https://linear.app/brutusvan/issue/VAN-46/install-swiveling-front-seats).

## Goal

Make both front seats swivel toward the living space in the 2017 European Ford Transit L3H3 AWD.

## Work

1. Inspect and photograph the existing driver seat, passenger double bench, seat-belt attachments, wiring, and handbrake location.
2. Find a compatible single passenger seat, with suitable belt and electrical equipment.
3. Select **driver and passenger swivel bases** for the specific 2017 Transit; verify side, mounting pattern, mounting height, crash/approval documentation, and clearance.
4. Select and install a **driver-side handbrake lowering kit or approved relocation solution** compatible with the swivel base. Confirm full brake travel, holding function, and operation.
5. Fit the bases and passenger seat, protecting any seat wiring; test travel locks, belts, seat adjustments, door/steering wheel clearance, and full swivel travel.
6. Confirm Finnish inspection/documentation requirements before permanent modification.

- [FarOutRide](https://faroutride.com/) as a reference for Transit swivel installations. **Do not assume North American Transit fitment applies** to this European AWD model.
- Confirm the swivel mounting height relative to the high roof and eventual cab-to-living-area access.

## Acceptance

Both seats face forward and lock securely for driving, swivel as intended when parked, handbrake and restraints operate normally, and inspection/approval questions are resolved.
