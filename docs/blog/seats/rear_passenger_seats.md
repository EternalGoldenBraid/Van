# Cargo-area passenger seats — VAN-47

**Status:** Planned / feasibility not established.
**Linear:** [VAN-47](https://linear.app/brutusvan/issue/VAN-47/add-one-or-two-approved-passenger-seats-in-cargo-area).

## Goal

Provide **one or two additional registered travel seats** in the cargo/living area of the 2017 Ford Transit L3H3 AWD. Seat count and position are undecided.

## Decision gates

1. Check the vehicle's existing registration class, approved passenger capacity, and original vehicle-tax treatment.
2. Before buying seats or modifying the floor, ask a Finnish modification-inspection station (*muutoskatsastus*) which seats, mounting/anchorage evidence, seat-belt arrangements, and certificates would be accepted.
3. Establish whether adding seats changes vehicle classification or causes additional **autovero**. Record the actual decision and costs.
4. Decide between one and two seats only after the interior layout is surveyed and the required space, access, and anchor points are known.

## Work — conditional on feasibility

- Select a compliant seat/seat-belt/anchorage system and installation specialist or verified mounting method.
- Determine position and orientation, access and emergency exits, headroom, payload, and interactions with floor framing, insulation, and future living-space fittings.
- Document existing vehicle structure, required reinforcement, and approved attachment method before any drilling.
- Install the seat system and complete any required inspection and registration changes before carrying passengers.
