# Wood Stove

**One part of the van build, updated 2026-10-23.** This section tells the story
of choosing and installing a small wood stove in a Ford Transit L3H3.
The stove, silicone roof
boot and three main chimney components are ordered. Nothing here claims that
the installation is finished, approved or proven in use.
Adhesives/sealants, brackets, clamps, fixings and other attachment materials
still need selecting, pricing and obtaining; their costs will be added as
the installation develops.

## Build Story Drafts

1. [What I want from a wood stove](story/01-what-the-stove-is-for.md):
   heat, cooking, atmosphere and the limits of a small insulated space.
2. [Choosing the stove](story/02-choosing-the-stove.md):
   why Boutique Traditional, and which uncertainties I accepted.
3. [The chimney became its own project](story/03-sourcing-the-chimney.md):
   choosing compatible components, finding Finland delivery and placing orders.

The [floor work plan](../floor/index.md) is a separate build stage. It covers
the immediate work, including the replacement-air vent, and the handover to
stove installation planning.

These are working drafts, not a finished installation guide. Later chapters
will cover the measured layout, hearth and shielding, roof passage, supports,
installation and observed operation when there is evidence to write them.

currency, tax basis and delivery assumptions. Unknown amounts stay unknown,
not zero. Update the cost ledger when invoices, refunds or further purchases
are available; the story links to it rather than maintaining another budget.
