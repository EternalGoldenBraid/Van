# 2. Choosing The Stove

**Draft, 2026-10-21.** Boutique Camping Traditional was ordered on
2026-10-19. Delivery and operating results are not yet confirmed.

## The Choice Was Not Just About Kilowatts

I compared compact stoves including Cubic Mini, Swiss Tiny Stove, Outbacker,
Nortent and CanvasCamp Bison. There was no single specification that decided
it. The tradeoff was between heat, the view of the fire, cooktop space, a rigid
body and the space taken up by the complete installation.

The [research notes](../research.md#retained-alternatives) retain the
alternatives and their sources. They are not a table of equally verified
performance figures: some manufacturers publish output ranges, while others
leave important dimensions or low-output behaviour unclear.

## Why Boutique Traditional

Boutique offered the combination I wanted: a broad cooktop, a large front
window and a substantial rigid body, with solid sides rather than glass all
around. The listing gives a 19 kg stove with a 2 mm steel body and top.

It also comes with six single-wall flue sections and other basic parts. That
does not make it a complete van installation kit. In particular, its supplied
tent-oriented flue arrangement is not the same thing as a fire-safe,
weatherproof passage through an insulated metal roof.

## The Risk I Accepted

I still do not know its clean minimum output in this van. Owner reviews from
tents and sheds do not settle that, and external dimensions do not reveal
the usable firebox volume.

Choosing it therefore includes a risk: small intermittent fires may work
well, or the stove may prove a poor match. Resale is the accepted fallback,
not an assurance of recovering the cost. The manufacturer's unwanted-return
policy is for unused, unassembled goods, so a firing trial cannot be treated
as a normal return opportunity.
https://boutiquecamping.com/en-eu/policies/refund-policy

The same caution applies to the chimney connection. Nominal 80 mm parts from
different product lines are not proof of a physically compatible joint. I
accepted ordering the selected connector before measuring the actual mating
ends; it still has to fit correctly before use.

## What It Cost

The earlier stove listing was **EUR 355.00**. The actual delivered/paid amount
has not been recorded, so that figure is a historical price, not a claimed
receipt total. Delivery, taxes and any import or handling charges need the
order confirmation or invoice.

That gap stays explicit in the [shopping list](../shopping_list_boutique.md). It would be easy to
make the project look cheaper by treating an unknown delivery charge as zero,
or by counting only the stove. Neither would be an honest account.
