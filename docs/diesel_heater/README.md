# Diesel heater — installation notes

**Status (2026-10-25):** Relocation in progress; not yet verified or commissioned. This document records the current physical build, not an approved installation design.

## Existing equipment and purpose

- Existing heater: **PLANAR-44D-12-GP-P**, 12 V diesel air heater, nominal 4 kW, removed from this Ford Transit during underside cleaning.
- Immediate aim: dry the van interior; a smaller (~2 kW) diesel heater may replace it later.
- Intend to reuse the existing fuel pump, fuel line, and exhaust where suitable. The actual condition and routing remain to be confirmed.

## Planned position

- Proposed location: recessed **sliding-door step well**, with the heater laid on its side. Placement inspired by Mispronounced Adventures' MAN TGE / VW Crafter build.
- Manufacturer mounting orientation **must be checked against the exact PLANAR 44D manual and heater body before drilling**; similarity to a newer Autoterm AIR-4D does not alone establish equivalence.
- Floor penetration locations are not finalized. Use a verified heater-specific hole pattern/template and inspect both faces of the metal before cutting. Check clearance to structure, wiring, fuel lines, insulation and the exhaust beneath the step.
- Plan is to avoid the previous large mounting-plate opening, using appropriately spaced smaller penetrations instead if this is permitted by the manufacturer.

## Mounting hardware and exhaust

- The original backing/mounting plate was removed by cutting the plate because the exhaust connection would not release. **The heater outlet itself was not cut.**
- Existing gasket remains present. There are shallow-looking edge defects; condition and sealing adequacy **have not been independently verified**. Do not fill or repair it with arbitrary adhesive without manufacturer approval.
- Original corrugated exhaust remains firmly attached at the heater outlet. Proposed work involves cutting the exhaust farther downstream near the muffler, instead of forcing the heater connection.
- A spare metal tube has been trial-fitted as a joining sleeve. Reported measurements: about **22 mm internal diameter, 1 mm wall thickness, 24 mm external diameter**. Material/coating and exhaust suitability are **unknown**; fit alone does not establish suitability or gas-tightness. Do not treat this as a validated connector.
- Mechanical retention, exhaust-rated joint, sealing, thermal clearances, and safe routing outside the van are **unresolved**. Do not use indoor CO readings as a substitute for validating the exhaust connection or heater combustion. Consult the heater maker or qualified installer before operation.
