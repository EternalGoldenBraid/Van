# Diesel heater — Planar 44D relocation

**Status (2026-10-25):** Installation in progress.

## Setup

- **PLANAR-44D-12-GP-P**, 12 V, 4 kW, removed from the Transit during underside cleaning.
- Initial purpose: dry the van interior. May later replace with a ~2 kW heater.
- Reusing the existing fuel pump, line and as much of the exhaust as possible.

## Placement and holes

- Relocating to the **sliding-door step well**, sideways, inspired by Mispronounced Adventures' MAN TGE / Crafter installation.
- Confirm the allowed sideways orientation against the Planar 44D mounting diagram before marking the holes.
- Aim for individual penetrations for fuel, combustion air and exhaust rather than the old large mounting-plate opening.
- Mark from the heater/template, then check underside access and drill.
- A step drill or metal hole saw is under consideration for the floor holes.

## Mounting and exhaust

- Original backing plate cut off because the exhaust would not separate from the heater outlet; heater outlet left intact.
- Existing gasket retained; shallow marks at its outer edge.
- Original corrugated exhaust remains stuck to the heater. Plan is to split it farther downstream, near the muffler.
- Trial-fitted metal joining tube: ~22 mm ID, ~1 mm wall. Nominal OD would be 24 mm; **measure actual OD** (23 mm was suggested in the review). Material/coating unknown.
- Final exhaust joint, clamps and mounting holes still to be completed and inspected.
