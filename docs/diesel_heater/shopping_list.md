## Already available / not buying for this work package

- Existing metal files.
- Existing heater, fuel pump, fuel line and exhaust.
- Homemade exhaust joining sleeve; no sleeve or exhaust clamps on this shopping list.
- Cutting oil is optional, not a planned purchase.
- Roof-fan-specific coatings and installation parts: shop separately later in the week, possibly Motonet.

## Check before shopping

- Maximum hole diameter on the physical heater/template (the Bosch step drill stops at 30 mm).
- Existing primer/topcoat supply before purchasing duplicates.
