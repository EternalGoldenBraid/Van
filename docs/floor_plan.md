## Goal (Floor Only)
- Seal old pop-rivet holes with the chosen bolt/washer/rubber assembly.
- Stop drafts (convection) and reduce noise.
- Install insulated, screw-down floor that stays serviceable.

## Planned Floor Stack (v1)
1. Clean + rust treatment + paint on exposed metal (especially around holes).
2. Butyl damping patches on large flat metal areas (optional).
3. Closed-cell foam (CCF) layer.
4. Battens grid (glued), with CCF cut out under battens so wood bonds to metal.
5. XPS infill boards (Topway heating boards are acceptable even if you do not install heating yet).
6. Plywood subfloor, screwed into battens.
7. Later: decide stove position; consider through-bolting with backing plates.

## Current Work Order
1. Prepare and protect the remaining hole edges and seal the holes using the
   assembly in the sealing logbook; preserve factory drains and access points.
2. Confirm coatings have cured and seals are leak-tight.
3. Install and fix the floor stack.
4. Install wall framing that bears on or prevents removal of the floor.
5. When insulating each door, inspect and treat any visible corrosion while the
   interior remains accessible; preserve its drains and mechanisms.
6. Have the company complete the agreed exterior-only underside treatment on
   the reconfirmed date.

## Accepted Tradeoff

Finishing the interior before professional treatment means the company cannot apply its full
interior/cavity treatment. This gives up professional coverage of seams and
enclosed areas that become inaccessible behind the conversion.

General visible interior floor and wall treatment is complete; the remaining
floor holes still require local preparation and sealing. Door interiors will
be handled during their insulation. Continuing the build after sealing is not
equivalent to the company's complete interior treatment.
