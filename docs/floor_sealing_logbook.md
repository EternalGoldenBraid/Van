## Current Direction

Protect the steel that will become inaccessible beneath the seals before
assembly. The user's chosen stack, inside to outside, is:

`bolt -> washer -> rubber seal -> van floor -> rubber seal -> washer -> nut`

The user has already cut seals from bike inner tubing and owns CRC Rust Seal
750 ml. Brush away loose rust/flaking coating on both sides and the hole edge
with the brass wire brush; abrade stubborn rust as needed, clean, and dry.
Apply the converter according to its label where rust remains. Let the coating
cure before clamping; tighten without extruding rubber or deforming the sheet.
Check for leaks before covering the floor.

## Findings And Sources

- **Retailer claim:** [CRC Rust Seal at Puuilo][crc] forms a protective layer,
  works without paint, and is paintable after two hours. This is not a verified
  cure time for clamping seals. Primer over this product is not confirmed
  necessary; compatibility with a particular primer remains unverified.
- **Selected, not bought:** [Tikkurila Rostex Super Akva, light grey, at
  K-Rauta][krauta], selecting the small 1/3 l option. Use the
  [shared heater/fan coating kit](diesel_heater/shopping_list.md#shared-coatings-and-preparation)
  with Panssari Akva topcoat; no additional set. Store price/stock unverified.
- **Manufacturer instructions:** [Rostex Super Akva datasheet][primer] permits
  brush application on prepared steel/galvanised steel. Use on clean fresh
  cut edges without converter; finish with a compatible water-based topcoat,
  such as Panssari Akva. Do not assume Maston spray topcoat compatibility.
  Overcoat after four hours at >=10 C and <=80% relative humidity; otherwise
  the next day. Application requires >=3 C, humidity <80%, and a dry surface
  warmer than the air. Final hardness takes about two weeks.
- Inner-tube rubber composition/durability is unverified; keep incompatible
  solvents and rustproofing off it. Ordinary rubber and this primer are not
  specified for hot flue parts; follow the [stove logbook][stove].
