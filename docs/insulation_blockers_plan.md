### Thermal Decision

The selected Autoikkuna rear windows and the previously considered
[passenger-side OEM window](https://www.autoikkuna.com/product/192/sivuikkuna-oikea-tumma-ford-transit-2014-)
are large, bonded, single-pane automotive glass. They have no insulating
cavity. Nomadic Ark ACG windows have an outer tempered-glass pane, inner
acrylic pane, argon-filled cavity, and thermally broken frame.

For equal area and airtight installation, the Nomadic Ark construction should
lose substantially less heat. No tested whole-window U-values were found, so
the exact improvement is not yet verified. Smaller Nomadic Ark windows improve
the result further by reducing glazed area.

The frame's thermal break is built into the frame and interrupts the
inside-to-outside metal path. The mounting sealant or gasket is primarily a
weather seal; do not assume it provides the thermal break. A removable
closed-cell-foam cover can extend over the glass and interior frame, provided
it does not obstruct latches, seals, drainage, or ventilation and can be
removed to dry condensation.

### Winter Door Strategy

Use the insulated, windowless sliding door as the normal winter entrance. Keep
the rear doors closed and cover both large fixed panes with removable,
close-fitting insulated covers. This preserves the factory-size rear view in
warmer seasons without requiring a cover on the frequently used sliding door.

The covers should overlap the glass perimeter, fit without interfering with
door latches or seals, and be removable so the glass and frames can be checked
and dried.

### Driver-Side Location Gate

Before building its placeholder frame:

1. Mark both forward and rearward 900 x 450 mm candidates.
2. Check the bed/furniture plan, seated sightline, exterior seams, and access.
3. Check ribs and both sides of the panel before selecting the location.
4. Confirm the manufacturer's cutout dimensions and permitted wall curvature.
5. Build the frame for the required finished wall thickness.

The 900 x 450 mm size is nominal; do not cut from that rectangle without the
manufacturer's cutout drawing or physical template.
