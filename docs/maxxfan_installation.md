## Buy Elsewhere

- RV butyl bedding tape
  - Buy: 2 rolls.
  - Requirement: plain, soft, non-reinforced butyl tape, approximately 25-30
    mm wide and 3 mm thick, preferably 5 m per roll.
  - Do not substitute foil-faced, fabric-reinforced, roofing-underlay, or
    bathroom waterproofing tape.
- RV roof lap sealant
  - Buy: 1-2 cartridges.
  - Requirement: UV-resistant, self-leveling sealant confirmed compatible with
    the MaxxFan plastic flange and painted vehicle roof.
  - Use: cover the flange perimeter and all flange screw heads.
- Isopropyl alcohol and lint-free cloths
  - Buy: 1 container and sufficient cloths.
  - Use: clean the painted metal and flange as permitted by their
    manufacturers.
  - Keep acetone and other aggressive solvents away from the plastic flange.
- Interior support frame
  - Buy: dry timber or plywood as required.
  - Requirement: the ring must give a finished roof thickness of at least 25
    mm. XPS is not a structural clamping frame.
- Electrical supplies
  - Buy: as required by the electrical plan.
  - Requirement: stranded automotive cable, heat-shrink crimp connectors, and
    a fused 12 V circuit capable of at least 5 A.

K-Rauta's listed butyl tapes are construction-system products rather than RV
flange bedding tape, so they are intentionally excluded.

## Check Or Bring

- MaxxFan, receiving flange, interior garnish, supplied screws, connectors, and
  physical template
- drill/driver and jigsaw
- tape measure, square, marker, and centre punch
- hand screwdriver for final screw tightening
- wire stripper, ratchet crimper, heat gun, and multimeter
- gloves, hearing protection, stable access platform, and roof protection
- plastic sheet or cardboard, tape, and vacuum for catching all metal swarf
- clamps or weights for holding parts during dry fitting

## Butyl Levelling Method

1. Dry-fit the receiving flange over the chosen roof position.
2. Add short butyl strips only in the roof valleys until the flange sits flat
   without rocking.
3. Add one uninterrupted final butyl layer around the full flange perimeter.
4. Install the flange and tighten evenly by hand only until the butyl begins to
   squeeze out.
5. Stop if the flange starts to bow. Butyl is a compressible weather seal, not
   a rigid structural spacer.
6. Cover every flange screw head and the complete flange perimeter with the
   compatible RV lap sealant.
