# Shopping List

Scratch list for current van-build purchases. Check stock again before
travelling.

## Wall Framing Into Existing M6 Rivnuts

K-Rauta Palkane stock checked on 15 September 2026.

- [ ] [PROF M6 x 1000 mm zinc-plated threaded rod, grade 4.8](https://www.k-rauta.fi/tuote/kierretanko-prof-m6x1000-sahkosinkitty-48-din-976-1/6438313568537)
  - Palkane stock: 11
  - Buy one rod for up to nine approximately 100 mm studs.
  - Buy two rods if more than nine studs are required.
  - Measure the actual stack before cutting.
  - Intended for insulation-support framing, not seats, furniture, or cargo
    restraints.
- [ ] [M6 wide body washers, DIN 9021, zinc-plated, 200-pack](https://www.k-rauta.fi/tuote/korialuslevy-prof-din9021-m6-sahkosinkitty-200kpl/6438313615910)
  - Palkane stock: 1 pack
  - Buy one washer per stud.
  - Place the washer between the wooden beam and permanent nut.
- [ ] [M6 Nyloc self-locking nuts, DIN 985, zinc-plated, 200-pack](https://www.k-rauta.fi/tuote/lukkomutteri-nyloc-prof-din985-m6-i8i-sahkosinkitty-200kpl/6438313620600)
  - Palkane stock: 1 pack
  - Buy one permanent Nyloc nut per stud.
  - This replaces liquid threadlocker on the permanent tightening side.
- [ ] [Ordinary M6 nuts, DIN 934, A2, 10-pack](https://www.k-rauta.fi/tuote/kuusiomutteri-prof-din-934-a2-70-m6-10kpl/6438313204794)
  - Palkane stock: 14 packs
  - Buy only if two spare ordinary M6 nuts are not already available.
  - Reuse the same two nuts as jam nuts for installing every stud.
  - After fitting the beam, washer, and Nyloc nut, place the jam nuts beyond
    the Nyloc nut and hold the stud with them while tightening the Nyloc nut.
    Remove the jam nuts afterward.
- [ ] https://www.puuilo.fi/bergen-hankaussieni-sisal-3kpl
- [ ] T20/25 4-5mm wood screws

Permanent assembly:

- existing M6 rivnut in the van body
- threaded stud with approximately 6-9 mm engagement in the rivnut
- wooden beam
- wide M6 body washer
- permanent M6 Nyloc nut

Do not replace the existing M6 rivnuts with M8 merely because ready-made M6 x
100 mm bolts are difficult to find. Do not let the stud bottom out in the
rivnut, and do not tighten enough to crush the wood or distort the body sheet.
Allow enough temporary stud length beyond the Nyloc nut for the two jam nuts.
