## Current Direction

Daytime heat/cooking; diesel primary/overnight. Target clean output 1-2 kW;
Boutique's clean minimum is not established. Accept the possibility of small,
brisk, intermittent fires rather than continuous gentle heat. Do not achieve
low output by smouldering a large fuel charge.

Boutique was chosen for its broad cooktop, large front window and substantial
rigid body. Solid sides, not wraparound glass. An accessible Outbacker Vista
was not selected as an intermediate installation: even a successful trial
would not establish Boutique's behaviour and could require reinstalling the flue.

**Purchase risk:** if unsuitable, resale is the accepted fallback; price recovery
is unknown. Boutique's published unwanted-return policy requires unused,
unassembled goods. Do not assume a firing trial followed by a normal return.
https://boutiquecamping.com/en-eu/policies/refund-policy

**Cooking:** no built-in oven. Prefer usable cooktop space for one flat-bottom
Dutch oven, with stable support and room around the flue for the pot/lid.
Choose the pot to fit the selected stove later; exact pot sizing is not a
purchase blocker. Cooktop load capacity and actual fit remain unverified.
A small water tank is useful for hot water, not a cure for an oversized stove:
3 L heated from 20 C to 90 C stores only about 0.24 kWh, and releases heat
back into the van if left there.

## Mounting And Chimney Height

**Stove mounting height:** Tiny Stove describes about 50 cm as an ideal position
for room integration and viewing from a favourite seat. That passage does not
give a legal or combustion reason for the height. It is not a Boutique mounting
requirement. No fixed minimum mounting height for a Finnish van was established
in this research; vehicle-installation/approval requirements remain unverified.
https://www.tiny-stove.com/info/

Mounting height is a layout choice within the stove's required floor, wall and
ceiling clearances and a secure mounting arrangement. At the same chimney-top
height, a lower stove gives more vertical rise from outlet to terminal; raising
the stove shortens that rise. Draft depends on this rise and exhaust temperature,
not independently on distance from the floor. Heat distribution and loading/
cooking ergonomics also change with mounting height.

**Total chimney height:** Boutique lists a supplied 170 cm flue, but no verified
minimum chimney-height instruction was found. Do not use the supplied length
as a minimum requirement. Tiny recommends at least 1.5 m total pipe and an
initial vertical rise of at least 50 cm, while noting shorter total pipes can
work. Those are Tiny-specific recommendations, not instructions for Boutique.
https://boutiquecamping.com/en-eu/products/traditional-wood-burning-stove?view=v2
https://www.tiny-stove.com/info/

**Above the roof:** Tiny says at least 30 cm above the roof; its installation
page specifies above the vehicle's highest point. This is not a verified
Boutique termination height. No separate minimum length of pipe in cold outside
air was established for Boutique. The terminal still needs an appropriate
height and position relative to the roof, rack, panels, fan and opening windows
for wind exposure and smoke separation. Outside cooling reduces draft;
insulation helps preserve exhaust temperature.
https://www.tiny-stove.com/info/
https://www.tiny-stove.com/einbau/

**Driving height in Finland:** the current Road Traffic Act, section 126 and
appendix 7.2(a), gives 4.40 m as the maximum overall height of a car or trailer,
including when unladen or with an axle lift raised. This is vehicle height,
not a 4 m maximum chimney length. Include any chimney left installed for travel;
measure the actual van and check route clearances, since the legal maximum does
not guarantee passage under every obstacle. Operating chimney height and the
travel arrangement must be planned separately; this road-height rule does not
establish an acceptable stove mounting or chimney termination.
https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2018/729

## Installation Holds

- **Final dimensions are blocked on clearances and projections.** All figures
  above are published hardware dimensions or explicitly labelled estimates,
  not safe installation envelopes. Equal clearances are a comparison
  assumption only. Obtain
  deployed front/side drawings and model-specific body/flue clearances,
  including any permitted shielded reductions; allow door operation and
  loading space. Do not infer reduced clearances from Bison's vermiculite.
- Final height depends on hearth elevation, measured outlet-to-roof distance,
  transition lengths, chimney supports and required termination above roof.
  Neither supplied tent-flue height nor shipping-box size settles it.
- Verify adapter fit, joint direction, insertion depth and temperature suitability.
- Establish clean low-output suitability and draft for the complete chimney:
  diameter, height, insulation and bends matter; a connector alone is insufficient.
- Measure against [roof layout](../roof_layout_plan.md); specify system-specific
  penetration clearances, hearth/shields, supports and weather flashing.
  The matching Jeremias cap is the chosen starting route; resolve road-load
  retention for travel and a separate cold-stove closure. Flashing is not fire
  protection; preserve required airspaces.
- Provide make-up air and CO/smoke alarms. No MaxxFan extraction with a lit
  stove unless designed/tested for depressurization and smoke spillage.
