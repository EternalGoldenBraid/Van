# Wood Stove Logbook

**Updated:** 2026-10-06
**Status:** Nortent preferred; purchase and installation specification unconfirmed.

## Current Direction

- Prefer [rigid Nortent][nortent], not the foldable model: two side windows,
  secondary air/baffle, room-air intake, 2 mm body / 3 mm top, 17 kg complete,
  70 mm flue.
- Supervised daytime heat/cooking; diesel primary/overnight. Target clean
  output: 1-2 kW. Nortent's clean minimum and measured efficiency are unknown.
- Investigate single-wall pipe indoors where clearances allow, transitioning
  to a compatible insulated section before the ceiling/roof. This lowers
  surface temperature near combustibles and retains heat for draft.

## Findings And Sources

| Source | Finding / limitation |
| --- | --- |
| [Nortent][nortent] / [diagram][nortent-diagram] | 2.6 m tent flue; no insulated roof kit, hearth, or shield listed. Panel/door seals unverified. |
| [Cubic kit][cubic-kit] / [instructions][cubic-manual] | Legacy/non-certified system: 3-inch double-wall flue inside 5-inch insulated sleeve; sleeve starts 2 inches below ceiling. 7-inch hole leaves 1-inch **airspace**. New certified models differ; not Nortent dimensions. |
| [Swiss Tiny Stove fitting][tiny-roof] / [instructions][tiny-install] | 80 mm single-wall pipe; double-wall penetration/cooling plate. Replaces ordinary insulation within about 5 cm with wool/heat-resistant board. Not certified. |
| [Vantastic: Cubic Mini][vantastic] | Insulated double-wall flue plus surrounding wool; exact materials/clearances unverified. |
| [Leon: Outbacker Firebox][leon] | Insulated flue, restraint, make-up-air vent. Reports four years with previous installation; new build unfinished/leaking rain. |
| [Taylors: Outbacker oven][taylors] | Sectional pipe, boot, inner cover; insulated section/clearances unverified. |

**Do not mix systems:** wool packing and required airspace are not interchangeable.
A weather boot is not fire protection; `palovilla` alone is not a penetration.

## Open / Next

- Confirm clearances, seals, fixed-use restrictions, and compatible chimney parts.
- Specify shields, thermal hearth over plywood/XPS, supports, and driving/removal.
- Measure outlet-to-roof, roof build-up, and obstructions; [roof layout](roof_layout_plan.md).
- Next: obtain requirements/matched parts before non-returnable purchases or cutting.

**Safety:** CO/smoke alarms and adequate make-up air required. No MaxxFan
extraction with a lit stove unless the combined system has been designed and
tested for depressurization/spillage. Any water vessel must be vented,
unpressurised, independently supported, and secured/emptied for driving.
