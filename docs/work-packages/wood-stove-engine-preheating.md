# Wood-stove engine preheating — future concept

Status: **draft / future feature**. Not an implementation plan.

## Idea

Explore using heat from the ordinary wood stove to preheat the engine coolant of the 2017 Ford Transit. Consider a separate stove-side liquid circuit, a liquid-to-liquid heat exchanger, and potential integration near the existing Webasto coolant-heater plumbing. Do not assume the Webasto pump can run independently without verifying its configuration.

## Open questions

- Heat collection method and passive protection against boiling when power or circulation fails.
- Engine-side connection and pump operation, including coolant routing with engine off.
- Isolation when engine heating is unwanted.
- Underfloor freeze, road-damage and heat-loss protection.
