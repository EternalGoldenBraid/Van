### Materials shortlist - not ordered

- Support frame (korotusjalusta / tukirunko): steel angle or box section,
  not timber for this proposal. [Steel stock][steel-stock]; section sizes,
  joints and vehicle anchors still to specify. A 30 kg static load alone
  does not size the travel restraints (kuljetuskiinnitys).
- Insulating hearth board (tulisijan alustan lämmöneriste):
  calcium silicate (kalsiumsilikaattilevy). [Skamol 50 mm][hearth-board],
  1000 x 1220 mm: Lahdesjärvi showed 14 boards, EUR 129 each, 1 November;
  open today until 18:00. Candidate, not a verified hearth specification:
  check thermal properties and allowable loading before buying.
- Hearth support/top (alustan tukilevy / pintapelti): steel tray/support
  and load-spreading face around the insulating board; no unsupported
  board or concentrated stove-foot loads assumed acceptable.
  Separate [ember plate (kipinäpelti / eduspelti)][ember-plate] if needed;
  this thin sheet is not the insulating slab or structural support.
- Wall shields (seinän lämpösuojalevyt): [Warma][shield], 480 or
  730 x 980 mm, 1 mm steel, supplied 30 mm spacers. Keep the specified
  30 mm ventilation gaps to floor/ceiling/adjacent shields.
  Two ventilated layers per protected face are the layout candidate;
  size/count and mounting remain open.

[Finnish rescue-service guide][clearance-guide]: old, non-CE sauna stoves,
not a universal van rule. Its wall-clearance drawings show:
- unprotected: 500 mm
- single lightweight shield: 250 mm (50% reduction)
- double lightweight shield: 125 mm (75% total reduction)

Assembly: >= 1 mm corrosion-resistant metal or >= 7 mm noncombustible
fibre-cement; 30 mm wall/interlayer air gaps. Protect at least 400 mm
beyond stove outlines; door-front clearance remains 1000 mm.
No documented further reduction by adding more panels. These figures
do not establish Boutique's baseline or approve 125 mm in this van;
agree the proposed assembly/clearances before fixing the layout.
Ceiling, flue and floor protection require their own specifications.

[Vantastic reference][vantastic-shields]: welded metal support/base;
stone wall finish; two metal shield sheets with a stated 30 mm gap.
Useful construction precedent, not a verified inspection record or a
minimum clearance for this stove. Warma [mounting details][shield-details].

[Boutique manual][stove-manual]: 60 cm sides/top from tent fabric;
no van-specific hearth specification or shielded-clearance reduction.
Material selection does not settle stove-to-shield clearance, hearth
thickness/load capacity or safe fuel storage underneath.

Chimney support (savupiipun kannake): [DW-FU wall/ceiling holder][flue-holder]
matched to DN80 / 145 mm outer shell. Steel angle/channel can form the
vehicle-side attachment; anchors, weight support and thermal movement
still need a layout. Boot and joint bands are not structural supports.

Roof boot (silikoninen läpivientikaulus): [Master Flash method][boot-guide]
forms the base to the roof ribs, seals beneath, then mechanically fastens.
[Standard silicone base][boot-profile] conforms to metal roof profiles;
dry-fit the delivered boot. No fan-style butyl levelling layer specified.
Select compatible weather/temperature-rated silicone and fixings for
the actual thin-sheet/support build-up; sealant alone must not bridge ribs.

Design requirement: reduce cold-stove draught/heat loss; plan a compatible
flue damper (savupelti) or cold-only closure before final flue assembly.
Selection remains open, not omitted; no purchase needed today.
A damper reduces airflow loss, not conduction through the metal chimney.
[Finnish chimney regulation, section 7][damper-rule] requires residual
CO to escape even with a closed damper. Never seal a flue with fire/embers;
do not cut a valve into the insulated Jeremias section.
Do not run the MaxxFan in exhaust mode while the stove burns or embers
remain: depressurisation can draw smoke/CO into the van.
